apeCAD · Proposal · ADR 0031

APE identity for apeCAD

Mockups of the restyled scratchpad in APE paper and carbon, and one section rendered to APE drawing standards. Docs only: nothing under src/ changes until the stages in the ADR are scheduled.

01

Scratchpad, light and dark

Steel marks state (selection, active tool, modes that are on). Vest orange marks attention (snap, crossing, refusals). On the light canvas, section entities already wear their drawing ink.

02

Section C1, rendered to APE standards

ADR-005 pens and ink, ADR-007 Arial Narrow notes in (0, 0, 119), ADR-009 Bahnschrift title, ADR-010 Grey210 cut. English on the sheet, office tag structure, millimetres throughout.

  • Pens on screen without a plot scale: max(0.75, 5 × mm) px, so 0.10 → 0.75, 0.20 → 1.0, 0.30 → 1.5, 0.40 → 2.0, 0.50 → 2.5.
  • Ø → Φ is render-only. The document keeps apeConcrete's Ø20.
  • No Mc marks, no lengths. Marks and planillas stay with the Revit set.
  • Review highlight draws in vest orange over the drawing and never reaches DXF.
03
